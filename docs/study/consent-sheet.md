# OwnStudio study: information and consent

**Draft for the dry run.** Before the student session, check with the university's data protection officer (DPO) whether this needs their review, and translate into French.

## What this is

LAAS-CNRS is testing OwnStudio, a teaching tool for power electronics. You will do one exercise with it, for about an hour, then answer a short questionnaire. We are testing the tool, not you.

## What is recorded

- **By OwnStudio**, on this computer only:
  - when you start and finish each step of the exercise;
  - your answers to the questionnaire (ten statements, three short questions).
- **By the observer**, on paper: what you say aloud and what you do, written down as notes.

**Not recorded:** your name, your screen, your voice, your code. Nothing is sent over the internet.

Your records carry a code (for example PHD-3), not your name. The list linking codes to names stays with the facilitator, on paper, and is destroyed after the study.

## What it is used for

To improve OwnStudio and the exercise. Results are reported as totals and quotes without names.

## Your rights

Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time without giving a reason. You can also ask for your records to be deleted until the results are reported:
- in OwnStudio, **Withdraw** deletes them;
- after the session, ask the facilitator.

## Safety at the bench

The board is powered from a current-limited supply at 30 V. Only the facilitator switches the supply on and off. Do not touch the board, the wiring or the loads while it is powered.
